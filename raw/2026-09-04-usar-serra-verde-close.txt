USAR — major developments

1. Serra Verde acquisition officially CLOSED yesterday, announced today. USA Rare Earth now owns Serra Verde, giving it an operating upstream heavy-rare-earth asset in Brazil alongside USAR’s metals/alloys and planned U.S. magnet manufacturing. Serra Verde says it is the only scaled producer outside Asia of all four magnetic heavy rare-earth elements. 

Production targets:

* Pela Ema/Serra Verde: ~4,000 tpa TREO run-rate by end-2026
* Stage 2: ~6,400 tpa, commissioning expected within ~12 months
* Longer-term Phase 2 could roughly double mine production. 

2. Today’s 8-K contains a major dilution/capital-structure detail. USAR paid $300M cash + issued 126.849M new USAR shares for Serra Verde. For perspective, USAR’s H1 weighted-average share count was ~213.35M; pro forma combined weighted-average shares become ~340.20M. The merger issuance alone is equivalent to almost 60% of the H1 share base. 

There is also a potential stock-overhang angle: USAR agreed to register the merger shares for resale, and for certain recipients one-third is unlocked immediately, one-third after 90 days and one-third after 180 days. 

3. Serra Verde brings secured floating-rate debt into USAR. The DFC financing provides up to $565M, consisting of a $465M initial facility and $100M incremental tranche. The initial debt costs Term SOFR + 4%, can run as long as 15 years, and is secured by a first-priority lien over the Serra Verde acquisition vehicle and substantially all its assets. The $100M incremental piece was extinguished in connection with closing. 

That’s worth putting beside today’s bond work: USAR has transformed from mostly development-stage optionality into an operating, leveraged, vertically integrated rare-earth company.

4. The government-backed Serra Verde offtake is enormous. This was announced Aug. 24, but becomes much more relevant now that USAR actually owns Serra Verde:

* $1.55B SPV capitalization
* Department of War: $750M
* Tier-1 bank: up to $500M senior secured facility
* U.S. government forward purchase: ≥$300M over five years
* SPV purchases 100% of Serra Verde Phase-1 production
* Contract includes take-or-pay + floor pricing. 

Even more important, the underlying offtake runs potentially 20 years, with pricing based on annually escalated floor prices; economics above the floor are split 70% Serra Verde / 30% SPV counterparty. 

5. Today’s China news is almost perfectly timed for USAR. Reuters reports that some Chinese rare-earth suppliers have been refusing U.S. shipments despite possessing export licenses, apparently because of fear of repercussions from Beijing. The issue is now on the agenda ahead of Xi’s Sept. 24 Washington visit. 

That is directly thesis-positive for an ex-China integrated chain. USAR was up roughly 5% premarket, although much of that gain subsequently faded.

Investment read

This is materially positive strategically, but not unambiguously bullish for the stock.

The bullish change is substantial: USAR now has real heavy-REE production + government-backed offtake + floor pricing + downstream metals/magnets, exactly when Chinese supply reliability is again deteriorating.

The offset is equally real: 126.85M new shares, resale registration/lockup expirations, acquired floating-rate debt, and substantial execution requirements.

So the highest-value USAR metric now changes from “Can they build it?” to:

Serra Verde actual TREO production/ramp → realized offtake price → Stillwater magnet qualification/backlog → cash generation per diluted USAR share.
