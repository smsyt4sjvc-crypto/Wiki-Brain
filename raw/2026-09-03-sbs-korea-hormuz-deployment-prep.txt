SBS (ROK) defense correspondent + Reuters pickup + Blue House caveat — South Korea preparing Hormuz naval deployment. Pasted by Jake 2026-09-03 ~9:18pm PDT

SBS’s defense correspondent reports that a key South Korean Ministry of National Defense official told them on September 3 KST:

“We are making concrete preparations to deploy naval forces to the Strait of Hormuz.”

SBS specifically identifies the prospective force package as P-8 Poseidon maritime patrol aircraft, Navy UDT personnel operating as an EOD team, and a naval logistics-support ship. It also reports that discussions with other countries over ports of call and operational bases are already underway. 

SBS further reports exactly the policy reversal in your post: Seoul had previously intended to consider deployment after the U.S.-Iran war ended, whereas military preparations are now occurring while the conflict is ongoing. 

Reuters has subsequently picked it up and says multiple South Korean media outlets are reporting preparations, potentially for deployment before year-end, with Seoul discussing the scale and nature of its contribution with the U.S., U.K. and France. 

The caveat

Deployment has NOT formally been authorized yet. South Korea’s presidential office is deliberately pushing back on that part.

As of September 4 in Korea, the Blue House says “no final decision has been made” and that the issue remains under review. It also says discussions have not yet reached the formal parliamentary-approval stage. Any actual deployment would require the domestic process including government/NSC action and National Assembly consent.
