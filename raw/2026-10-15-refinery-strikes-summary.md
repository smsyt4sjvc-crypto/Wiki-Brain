- Oct 15 (confirmed): Ukrainian drones hit Rosneft's Ryazan refinery (~5% of national throughput, key Moscow supplier); governor reported a fire; first confirmed Russian energy-site strike since Trump's Sunday energy-ceasefire announcement (neither side fully confirmed it); Zelensky to FT: the three-day pause is over. Putin called recent hits insignificant and vowed a response. Russia claimed (Oct 14) attacks on Saratov + Ryazan that Ukraine denied; governors said no facility damage.

- Diesel/margins: Russian refinery activity at multi-decade lows, product exports down hundreds of kb/d; diesel cracks $80–100+/bbl in key markets; TotalEnergies and Shell flagged strong downstream; IEA: middle-distillate strain likely persists well into 2027; Trump has blamed Ukrainian strikes for high US diesel.
