# Who pays for a hit tanker — insurance-stack sources (pulled 2026-10-11 ~1:10pm PDT, Jake's Q "who pays for these ships? To fix them, cleanup etc.")

## Premiums / market
- **Canadian Underwriter, 2026-10-09 (Phil Porado), broker Alvarez:** "Before this big conflict, we were looking at [around] .25% of the insured value of vessels for hull and machinery [coverage]… And the latest numbers I'm seeing…[are] something between 7.5% and 10% – an increase of 30 to 40 times." Separate war-risk cover needed; non-damage business interruption (vessel trapped 6-8 months) "wouldn't be covered by war risk or hull and machinery policies." https://canadianunderwriter.ca/news/industry/no-end-in-sight-to-blockade-in-hormuz-what-happens-to-your-clients-marine-coverage-now/
- **Hellenic Shipping News / S&P Global Platts, 2026-10-05, Neil Roberts (head of marine & aviation, Lloyd's Market Association; chairs the Joint War Committee):** JWC expanded listed areas in the Black Sea, Red Sea, Persian Gulf, Arabian Sea this year; "If an area is listed and the vessel wishes to go there, yes, they should notify their underwriter… Otherwise, they won't have cover." Tanker operators/charterers face "a more than 40-fold increase in headline AWRP rates, calculated as a percentage of hull value, for transiting the Strait of Hormuz"; coverage periods shortened; fewer no-claim bonuses (up to 50% in peacetime). **IUMI "recently estimated that insurers could have lost $2 billion in the Middle East war."** Over 100 merchant ships attacked this year (IMO, governments, consultancies). **"In August, Omani authorities reported about 390 square kilometers of oil spilled from the sanctioned Suezmax Caroline Bezengi, which had been stranded after being damaged in an attack two months prior."** 1,226 tankers/LPG carriers sanctioned as of 8/31 (S&P Global Energy Horizons). https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/war-risk-insurance-costs-rise-as-sanctions-complicate-casualty-response/
- **Tehran Telegraph, 2026-10-08 (bot-authored; Iranian-named outlet — class 8):** relays Al Jazeera July citing S&P Global: premiums "from 1–3 percent of hull value to 7.5–10 percent"; late April ~5% vs 0.25% pre-war.
- **Howden Re, "Strait of Hormuz" report, 2026-03-27 (a reinsurance broker — class 8, gains from hardening rates):** "All cover is voyage-by-voyage as there are no annual war risk policies being written for Gulf." "Marine Hull and Cargo War represent 2-3% of the total marine insurance market." Mutually entered owners keep "Excess War P&I, with a limit of USD 500m excess of the underlying War Hull sum insured." Gard, Skuld, NorthStandard, London P&I Club, Steamship Mutual cancelled NON-mutual (fixed premium / charterers) entries. DFC facility: "public-private partnership between DFC, Chubb and reinsurers… Chubb, acting as lead underwriter… DFC does not directly underwrite… provides the $20bn rolling backstop"; covers war hull, war P&I (third-party liability, wreck removal, port damage), war cargo, and environmental damage. https://www.howdenre.com/sites/howdenre.howdenprod.com/files/2026-03/HowdenRe_Strait_of_Hormuz_report_March272026.pdf
- **LMA market statement, 2026-03-23:** war cover "remains available within the Lloyd's and London company market"; "Liability coverage through the P&I Clubs is non-cancellable and remains reinsured in the London market." https://lmalloyds.com/safety-concerns-not-insurance-availability-driving-reduced-vessel-traffic-in-the-strait-of-hormuz/
- **Reuters, 2026-03-02:** Gard, Skuld, NorthStandard, London P&I Club, American Club cancellations effective March 5. https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/ship-insurers-cancel-war-risk-cover-due-iran-conflict-2026-03-02/

## Who reimburses the premium — BIMCO primary
- **CONWARTIME 2025 (d):** "If the Vessel proceeds to or through or remains in an Area exposed to War Risks, the Charterers shall reimburse Insurance Costs to the Owners." **(e):** crew war bonus/additional wages "shall be reimbursed to the Owners by the Charterers." "Insurance Costs" = "any additional war risks premiums" + additional kidnap & ransom. https://www.bimco.org/contractual-affairs/bimco-clauses/current-clauses/war_risks_clause_for_time_charters_2025/
- **VOYWAR 2025 (e)(i):** annual war-risk premiums "shall be for their [Owners'] account"; **(e)(ii):** Charterers reimburse Insurance Costs for a listed-area voyage. https://www.bimco.org/contractual-affairs/bimco-clauses/current-clauses/war_risks_clause_for_voyage_charter_parties_2025/

## Oil-spill compensation — treaty primary (IOPC Funds text)
- **1992 CLC Art. III(2)(a):** "No liability for pollution damage shall attach to the owner if he proves that the damage: (a) resulted from an act of war, hostilities, civil war, insurrection…"
- **1992 Fund Convention Art. 4(2)(a):** "The Fund shall incur no obligation… if: (a) it proves that the pollution damage resulted from an act of war, hostilities, civil war or insurrection…" https://iopcfunds.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/WEB-IOPC-Text-of-Conventions-ENGLISH.pdf
