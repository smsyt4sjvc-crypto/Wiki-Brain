THE FOUR INTEL EXPLANATIONS, GRADED BY THE AUTHOR:
1. A FILING (real, verifiable, four weeks old): Intel priced 210,526,315 shares at $95.00 on
   10 August under Form 424B5 = exactly $20.0bn, with an option over another 31,578,947 shares.
   "A reason Intel is where it is rather than a reason Intel moved today."
2. A JUNE NUMBER WEARING TODAY'S CLOTHES: NVIDIA's stake is being quoted at about $30bn. That
   figure, $29,989,261,126, is the 13F valuation at 30 June and implies Intel at $139.63. Intel
   is $104.58. The same 214,776,632 shares are worth about $22.5bn now. NVIDIA paid $5.00bn at
   $23.28 in December, so it is up about 4.5x on cost AND about $7.5bn below the quoted mark.
   "Both of those are true. Only one is being said."
3. UNCONFIRMED: a press report sourced to the supply chain has Intel raising PC processor prices
   by about 10% in October. No company confirmation. "It may well be right. It is not evidence yet."
4. DENIED: Korean media reported ~31 August that SK hynix would use Intel Foundry for HBM4E base
   dies. SK HYNIX SAID IT IS NOT CURRENTLY CONSIDERING INTEL FOUNDRY FOR HBM4E PRODUCTION. The
   technical half is real and worth keeping: SK hynix validated its designs against Intel's
   EMIB-T at Hot Chips 2026. The commercial half is what the named customer has denied.

THE AUTHOR'S OWN PRIOR TEST, held to: "the thing that would change my view on Intel Foundry is one
line in the next 10-Q, external customer revenue with a customer named, because the segment booked
$5.77bn of revenue last quarter and $293m of it came from outside customers. Today the market is
trading a named customer. The customer says no."

HIS OBJECTION: "not that Intel should not be up... What I object to is the laundering. A June
valuation quoted as today's, and a denied report repeated as fact, stacked into a list that reads
like four confirmations."

WATCHING: whether Intel holds this into Friday (same test he set for the optical rotation
yesterday); whether SK hynix says anything further ("a denial that is still a denial in a month is
a different object from one that turns into an announcement"); and NVIDIA's next 13F, due in
NOVEMBER for the September quarter — "If Intel is still 47% of it after a quarter like this, the
position is a strategy. If it has been trimmed, it was a trade."
