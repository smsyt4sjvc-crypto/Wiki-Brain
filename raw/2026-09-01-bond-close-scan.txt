INTERPRETATION:

Today's fragility signal strengthened.

The important distinction is WHY.

Friday:
real rates exploded.

Monday:
long-end term premium kept rising.

Tuesday:
real yields were nearly unchanged,
but nominal yields rose again as oil and inflation compensation increased.

So the bond market is now being pressured from THREE directions:

FED / REAL RATE RISK
+
FISCAL / TERM-PREMIUM RISK
+
OIL / INFLATION RISK.

At the same time, AI financing continues to demand enormous amounts of duration capital.

Treasury needs:
$739B this quarter
+
$628B next quarter.

Corporates just issued a record:
$164B of IG debt in August.

And the AI system continues adding:
long-term leases
+ GPU-backed SPVs
+ customer payment extensions
+ private credit
+ residual-value guarantees
+ lessor guarantees
+ equity incentives
+ cloud financing facilities.

CREDIT HAS NOT BROKEN.

IG 80 bp and HY 263 bp are still very calm.

But two things changed enough to matter:

CCC OAS widened 16 bp.
MOVE jumped above 75.

That is the first hint that the Treasury shock may be beginning to bleed into the weaker parts of credit rather than remaining completely isolated in government bonds.

Tomorrow's ICE BofA publication is therefore unusually important.

If today's 4-6 bp Treasury selloff is accompanied by another meaningful HY/CCC OAS widening:
TRANSMISSION IS STARTING.

If OAS stays flat:
the system is still successfully absorbing higher rates.
