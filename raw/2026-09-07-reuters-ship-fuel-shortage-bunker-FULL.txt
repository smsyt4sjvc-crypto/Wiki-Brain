# Reuters — "Ship fuel shortage looms as refiners strained by war favour other products"
# Enes Tunagur (London) + Jeslyn Lerh (Singapore). Sept 6 2026 6:02pm PDT, updated ~Sept 7. Dateline LONDON, Sept 7.
# Editing: Alex Lawler, Dmitry Zhdannikov, Jan Harvey. FULL TEXT pasted by Jake 2026-09-07 ~8:08pm PT.

A shortage of fuel oil used in ships and power plants looms in the third quarter as refiners increasingly
squeezed by wars that have disrupted both crude processing and tanker traffic prioritise output of diesel
and other products at its expense.

While crude oil has avoided major price spikes in recent months, refined product prices have soared as
strikes damage refineries in Russia and the Middle East and curbs on ship traffic choke flows.
CHINA HAS ALSO CUT REFINING CAPACITY AND EXPORTS TO AVOID BURNING STOCKS.

Tightening supply threatens to further raise costs for shipowners and power generators already dealing with
war-related disruptions. Higher bunker fuel costs could also in turn feed into shipping rates.

Asia hardest-hit — most reliant on Gulf flows disrupted by the Iran war; Singapore, the world's largest
bunker hub, imports more than half its nearly 1 million bpd of demand (Kpler). Rystad has a similar outlook.

Deficit expected to hit 218,000 bpd in Q3 (Energy Aspects) — first shortfall since Q3 2025, when it was a
marginal 6,000 bpd.

Rystad's Valerie Panopio: "Due to the protracted supply disruption in the Middle East, we expect fuel oil
supply to remain critically tight in the third quarter."

Fuel oil joins GASOLINE, DIESEL and JET FUEL among refined products struggling to keep up with demand.
US DIESEL PRICES HIT RECORD HIGHS ON FRIDAY as renewed U.S.-Iran hostilities and Ukrainian attacks on
Russian refineries increased supply disruptions.

Refiners opting to produce other products to tap fatter profits has meant fuel oil has been hit harder.
NIGERIA'S 650,000 bpd DANGOTE REFINERY has ramped up diesel, gasoline and jet fuel exports while its
FUEL OIL EXPORTS HAVE DROPPED (Kpler).

Dangote and other refineries CAN USE FUEL OIL AS FEEDSTOCK IN SECONDARY REFINING UNITS to make other fuels.
Energy Aspects' Royston Huan: "Record-low gasoline and diesel inventories will incentivise refiners globally
to MAXIMISE SECONDARY UNIT RUNS WITH MORE FUEL OIL FEEDSTOCK BARRELS, in turn tightening fuel oil balances."

ARA independently-held GASOLINE stocks hit their lowest in nearly five years on Aug 27.
US EAST COAST DISTILLATE inventories (incl. diesel) fell to a RECORD LOW in the week to Aug 28.

PRICE UP 76% IN TOP HUB SINGAPORE
Stocks ~30% below three-year seasonal averages in Singapore, ARA and Fujairah (Reuters-compiled data).
Ships sailing longer routes to avoid BAB EL-MANDEB / the Red Sea due to Houthi threats ALSO INCREASE DEMAND
(Panopio).
VLSFO up 76% since the Iran war started to just under $825/metric ton, or $130/bbl, Singapore as of Sept 1
(ZeroNorth). Outstrips a 40% rise in Brent over the same period.

HITS TO RUSSIA, MIDDLE EAST OUTPUT
Russia fuel oil exports August: record low 591,000 bpd vs >860,000 bpd average in 2025 (Kpler, data to 2017).
Middle East fuel oil exports March-August: -45% y/y to 447,000 bpd average (Kpler).
Kuwait's AL-ZOUR (a top fuel oil exporter): one 26,000 bpd cargo since March vs ~191,000 bpd in Jan-Feb (Kpler).
