1. HORMUZ ESCALATION IS THE CLEAR #1 RISK — 10/10

2. EL-ERIAN IS EXPLICITLY MAKING OUR TREASURY-vs-AI-DEBT ARGUMENT — 9.5/10
Source/account: Mohamed El-Erian.
Core claim: governments, hyperscalers and corporations are issuing more bonds than the traditional reliable buyer base can comfortably absorb.
Confirmed vs opinion: the supply/demand interpretation is El-Erian’s analysis, not an established causal fact. But the backdrop is real: Friday’s 10Y finished around 4.78%, and this shortened week brings $58B 3Y Tuesday, $39B 10Y Wednesday and $22B 30Y Thursday before Friday CPI. 
Affected: TLT, banks, REITs, leveraged growth, ORCL/neocloud/data-center financing.
Thesis: Strongly strengthened. AI debt isn’t occurring in a vacuum; it is bidding for capital against sovereign supply.

3. IREN IS A NEAR-PERFECT JE​VONS/CAPITAL-CYCLE CASE STUDY — 9/10
Source: FT / IREN CEO Daniel Roberts.
Core claim: AI compute supply may never catch demand.
Confirmed: IREN plans to spend up to $30B by June 2027, has raised roughly $19B over the past year, and currently has about $7.6B of cash and $7.6B of debt, plus roughly $3.5B remaining under its share-sale program. It supplies Microsoft/Perplexity workloads and has Nvidia relationships across supplier/customer/investor roles. Roberts’s statement that supply may “never” catch demand is explicitly management opinion. 
Affected: IREN, NVDA, MSFT, neoclouds, power/data-center equipment.
Thesis: Both sides strengthen simultaneously: compute demand/Je​vons looks enormous, while financing intensity, leverage and dilution become increasingly important.

4. THE PHYSICAL AI TRADE IS RIPPING IN ASIA DESPITE RATES/OIL — 8.5/10
Source: AP/Barron’s/global-market feeds.
South Korea’s Kospi +4.6%, SK Hynix +8.1–8.3%, Samsung +5.7%, and Japan’s Nikkei about +2.1% today, driven heavily by semiconductor/memory enthusiasm. 
Affected: MU, NVDA, AVGO, TSM, AMAT/LRCX/KLAC, memory/HBM complex.
Thesis: Supports your current rotation read: investors are still willing to pay for physical compute/memory demand even while software and long-duration valuations face the rate hurdle.

5. DATA-CENTER POWER/POLITICAL BOTTLENECK IS SPREADING GLOBALLY — 8/10
Source: Bloomberg layer / Thailand government reporting.
Thailand has suspended 49 proposed data centers while it develops new rules around power, water and local impacts. The government approved 88 AI/data-center projects totaling 886B baht in H1 alone, already exceeding all of 2025 tech investment, and is considering higher electricity rates for large data centers. 
Affected: hyperscalers/neoclouds; bullish relative read-through for power-rich jurisdictions, behind-the-meter generation and infrastructure owners.
Thesis: Strongly supports the power-is-the-scarce-resource thesis. Compute demand can be abundant while permitted energized megawatts remain scarce.
