# Russian diesel licence (OFAC GL 135) vs US law — sources pulled 2026-10-11 ~11:00pm PDT (Jake: "the questionable legal grounds to even buy Russian diesel")

- **Business Upturn, 2026-10-10 (Vipul Sipani), "Trump's Russian diesel licence collides with a 500% duty deadline on October 18":**
  - GL 135 (10/9): authorises sale, delivery, offloading and importation of Russian diesel, incl. into the US, until 12:01am ET 4/7/2027.
  - **Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act (signed 9/18/2026; Public Law 119-111 per WatchfulEye) §112:** the president "shall, notwithstanding any other provision of law, increase the rate of duty for all goods, including oil, natural gas, liquefied natural gas, petroleum, petroleum products… imported into the United States from the Russian Federation to a rate of up to 500 percent ad valorem" within 30 days of enactment. **Day 30 = Sun 10/18; several law firms read the operative date as Mon 10/19.** "Shall" — discretion is over how high, not whether.
  - §114(h)(1) preserves licences issued BEFORE 9/18; GL 135 (10/9) falls outside it.
  - §115 waiver: requires a written national-interest certification + report to Congress first; the carve-out from reporting covers §113(b) rate changes only, not §112 duties. "As of Saturday no such filing has been reported."
  - **Ending Importation of Russian Oil Act of 2022 (Public Law 117-109) §2** bans imports of Russian-origin HTS Chapter 27 goods (diesel is Chapter 27); **GL 135 does not cite it**; §3 termination procedure: presidential certification, ≥45 days prior consultation with committees, 90-day review window.
  - §113 (secondary tariffs on top-5 buyers of Russian crude/gas, up to 100%) triggers on new purchases on/after day 30; requires 10 days' written notice to committees before any duty.
- **WatchfulEye, 2026-10-10:** GL 135 digitally signed 2:43pm ET, Trump's post 2:46pm ET; §112 deadline 18 October "shall… up to 500 percent"; §115 waiver needs certification + report; §117 ending sanctions requires a peace agreement "accepted by the free and independent Government of Ukraine" + 30-day congressional review. AP: "a stunning reversal"; Kremlin confirmed willingness, no quantities.

The Administration has also failed to consult with Congress or submit any explanation to justify its decision as required by statute."
