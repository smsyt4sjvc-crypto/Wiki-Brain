## IFR (International Financing Review), Gareth Gore, "Chips with everything: Broadcom backstops US$42bn funding for Anthropic", published 2026-10-09 13:10 UTC (6:10am PT)
https://www.ifre.com/loans/2496132/chips-with-everything-broadcom-backstops-us42bn-funding-for-anthropic
- "The syndication of US$42bn of senior secured loans backstopped by Broadcom began on Monday. The equipment lease financing is to help Broadcom's customer Anthropic pay for a massive order of its custom-made chips…"
- "The deal is split evenly between two US$21bn tranches and forms part of a wider US$61.5bn financing package to fund the Anthropic order, with a US$1.5bn term loan also being syndicated plus an US$18bn junior tranche set to hit markets over the next few months."
- "Broadcom will partially guarantee senior debt issued by a special purpose vehicle, which will buy the chips and lease them to Anthropic. The backstop will kick in if Anthropic goes bust or falls into difficulties."
- "One tranche of the loan pays a margin of 187.5bp over SOFR while the second pays 150bp over. Both tranches mature on September 30 2033. The difference in pricing may reflect different guarantee structures."
- "Anthropic has agreed to issue up to US$42bn of convertible notes to Broadcom…" · "The company has put in place a US$15bn revolving credit facility with banks ahead of the IPO and… is likely to seek an investment-grade rating when listed. That could open the door to the sale of the remaining US$18bn of junior debt…"
- (fragmentary in the search highlight: an analyst quote on whether "debt's tenor and amortisation match the equipment's economic life"; Broadcom "could face calls… its support just as… chip sales weaken.")

## Reuters EXCLUSIVE 2026-10-01 (Echo Wang, Milana Vinn, Max Cherney), "Broadcom to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion to lease its chips, filing says"
- Facility could finance about one-third of Anthropic's $125.2B five-year TPU lease commitment; Broadcom could designate a financing partner; notes convertible into Anthropic equity; no notes expected to be sold before the IPO.
- "Anthropic said it deposited cash into a restricted account for Broadcom's benefit in April 2026 and may be required to contribute additional amounts in certain circumstances."
- "certain payment or performance defaults could make a substantial portion of its lease obligations immediately due while limiting its ability to use the $42 billion financing facility to cover those payments."
- Prospectus flags "potential conflicts of interest" in Broadcom's dual supplier/lender role.
