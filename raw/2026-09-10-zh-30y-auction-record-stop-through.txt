      Record-Breaking 30Y Auction Sees Huge
      Stop Through, 2nd Highest Foreign
      Demand On Record
      One day after a blowout 10Y auction, which saw massive buyside demand thanks to the
      surge in yields earlier after the Bessent buyback disappointed which led to a huge concession
      into yesterday';s 10Y auction, moments ago we got the week's final coupon auctio when the
      Treasury sold $22BN in 30Y paper in an auction that was on the verge of blowing away
      many records.

      Starting at the top, the auction priced at a high yield of 5.308%, up from 5.212% in August
      and the 5th consecutive 30Y auction pricing above 5%. It was also the highest yield going
      back all the way to August 2001. Just as notably, the auction stopped through the When
      Issued 5.335% by a whopping 2.7bps, the second highest stop through on record!




      The bid to cover was likely impressive, surging to 2.612 from 2.392, the highest going back
      to February.

      The internals were even more impressive: Indirects were awarded a whopping 79.5%, up
      from 66.9% in August and the second highest on record (only Oct 2024 was higher).
