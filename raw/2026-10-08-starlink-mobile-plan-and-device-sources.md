## Wireless Estimator, "SpaceX wants to be a fourth major network, but it's building around towers, not on them" (2026-08-06)
- "SpaceX has described a terrestrial buildout strategy that houses small cellular radios alongside its existing Starlink
  broadband dishes, rather than building new macro towers or requiring hardware at every customer's home."
- Shotwell (Q2 call, first as a public company): the EchoStar spectrum "does have terrestrial components, so we definitely
  intend to build out the terrestrial component." 65 MHz via EchoStar vs ~5 MHz today via carrier partnerships.
- Shotwell: housing "these little femtocells" inside the hardware that holds a Starlink dish; won't need to spend "10 or many
  billions of dollars" upfront. Musk: the alternative to very expensive, hard-to-site large base stations is many small ones.
- "Read plainly, that's SpaceX describing a strategy to avoid conventional macro tower infrastructure, not lease into it."
- Shotwell: the Big Three collect ~$600B/yr in the US; expects to "acquire quite a few" customers. Commercial service targeted
  end of next year (2027).
- Tower REITs reported in line/ahead and rose after; SBA CEO Brendan Cavanagh: SBA in talks with more than one satellite
  company about deploying terrestrial g[ear] (excerpt truncated).

## Android Authority, "SpaceX reveals how Starlink Mobile plans to take on AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile" (2026-08-05, via Fierce Network)
- Same Shotwell quote; hybrid space + ground "true mobile service"; next-gen satellites make it "100 times better"; launch by end
  of next year. Musk: distributed network of smaller base stations integrated with existing Starlink installations on homes
  and businesses. "SpaceX still owns far less spectrum than AT&T, Verizon, or T-Mobile."

## PhoneArena, "Elon Musk may be making a Starlink mobile device with satellite connectivity, but it's not a phone" (2026-02-06)
- Reuters, three sources: SpaceX developing a mobile device that connects to Starlink, to challenge smartphones.
- Musk on X: "We are not developing a phone." (did not deny a device)
- Musk earlier, on a Starlink phone: "Optimized purely for running max performance/watt neural nets."
- Context: OpenAI partnering with Jony Ive on a device; Apple reportedly considering an AI pin; Humane AI Pin flopped.

## TechRadar (2026-10-02), Axios (2026-09-29), Inc. (2026-09-30)
- AT&T CEO John Stankey: SpaceX's plan "not a viable strategy"; "nothing beats" fiber; "you can't radiate cellular signals from
  somebody's house without their permission, unless somebody changes the law on that."
- AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon launched a joint venture to cover dead zones with satellites.
- Bernstein (Madison Rezaei): Starlink Mobile "may fill a meaningful gap… (remote, rural, maritime, disaster scenarios)" but "a
  long way from replacing terrestrial carrier networks."
