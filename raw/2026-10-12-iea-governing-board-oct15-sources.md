# IEA Governing Board, 15 October 2026 — what is on the table (sources pulled 2026-10-12 ~12:15am PDT, Jake: "Tell me about the IEA meeting")

- **IEA primary (10/11 12:29 UTC):** Birol letter "Ahead of the IEA Governing Board meeting on 15 October": (1) members not yet fully delivered on the **March 2026 Collective Action** should accelerate remaining releases, prioritising diesel; (2) all members encouraged to assess whether **additional** diesel releases are necessary and set out "scale, timing and supplemental nature"; the 15 October meeting "will provide a valuable opportunity to take stock of these efforts." (`raw/2026-10-11-zh-oil-rises-reopen-iea-letter.md`)
- **IEA primary (10/7 14:19 UTC = 7:19am PT):** members "expressed support for accelerating the oil stock releases announced in the Collective Action of March 2026"; prioritise diesel; review "at the next scheduled meeting of the IEA Governing Board, which will take place next week."
- **Reuters 10/6 (Rome/London; via Investing.com, MarketScreener, Euronext):** G7 agreed Fri 10/2 to release **100M barrels of diesel and crude**, no breakdown by product or country; the IEA Governing Board "is expected to decide the details… at an **October 14-15** board meeting, two people close to the matter said"; Italy in; EU oil coordination group met Wed 10/7; **Vitol CEO Russell Hardy:** "It wasn't a fully transparent announcement about exactly what was being released where… over the next two weeks, we'll see tenders… those tenders will tell us exactly how much oil and in which location."
- **Euronews exclusive 10/8 (Pacheco), internal document:** the G7's up-to-100M "aims to complete the outstanding portion of the 400-million-barrel emergency release coordinated by the IEA in March," **not a new 100M on top**; G7 2 October: "a 100 million barrel coordinated release over four months, including a substantial front-loaded diesel release within the first 20 days," linked to "immediate and full implementation" of March commitments; Birol: Middle Eastern crude flows recovering significantly, refined products "severely constrained"; IEA data show "a real physical disruption in diesel supply"; members hold ~1.1B bbl public stocks incl. >200M bbl diesel.
- **Reuters via Rig & Refinery 10/10:** two EU diplomats — the Governing Board met **informally Wed 10/7** on the G7 plan; split crude/diesel, country allocations and window not disclosed; consultative, not decisive.
- **Fortune 10/12 (Occhino):** JPMorgan (via Reuters) read 10/7 as "an acceleration of barrels already promised, not a fresh intervention"; public stocks ~1.1B bbl (from >1.2B in March); Aramco's Nasser: >1B bbl pulled from stockpiles worldwide; **US SPR ~284M bbl, lowest since 1982, heading to ~243M** when the 172M US share is delivered; Wright warns too-deep draws could damage caverns; the G7 asked the IEA for a follow-up report within 20 days incl. how to replenish stocks.
- **GAO-26-106918 (primary, 5/29/2026):** US share of an IEA collective action "just over 40 percent"; March 2026 US share 172M bbl; SPR held just over 413M bbl (Dec 2025); effective drawdown and fill capability 61% and 56% of design (Dec 2025); aging infrastructure "will increasingly limit the SPR's capability to meet fill or drawdown directives."
- **OilPrice 10/11 (Kern):** DOE runs the 172M as **loans repaid with 18–28% interest in barrels**, some returns not due until late 2028; design drawdown 4.415 mb/d vs fill 0.785 mb/d; statutory floor 252.4M bbl applies to limited drawdowns only; ~$20B to buy back to the ~680M effective peak at ~$80. (Says the G7 100M is "on top of" March — contradicted by Euronews' document and JPMorgan.)
- **AFP via Le Temps 10/11:** 325M of the 400M March pledge delivered by end of last week.
