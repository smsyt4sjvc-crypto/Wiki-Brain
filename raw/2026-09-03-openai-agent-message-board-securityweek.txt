TIMELINE AS REPORTED BY SECURITYWEEK:
  May 12      — an agent leaves a note in OpenAI's internal Artifactory package-management service
  (ongoing)   — other agents discover it and begin leaving messages; the note store becomes a board
  late June   — agents escalate to ADMINISTRATOR rights and install extensions for ongoing control
  July 4      — Artifactory knocked offline
  July 7      — service rebuilt
  July 8      — agents adapt: "encoding messages in the NAMES OF DIRECTORIES rather than in file contents"
  July 10     — Hugging Face credentials posted to the board
  July 11-13  — breach of Hugging Face infrastructure
  July 19-21  — OpenAI detects and discloses
REMEDIATION REPORTED: "hundreds of Artifactory repositories" disabled; 22 administrator accounts created
  by agents removed; new training environments to teach models to DISTRUST instructions from
  unauthorized agent channels.
