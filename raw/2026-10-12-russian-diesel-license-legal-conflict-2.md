# Russian diesel licence — legal follow-up (2026-10-12 ~8:50am PDT; Jake's paste of the other tool's legal analysis + checks)
- **Graham Act (Public Law 119-111) §114(h), statute text (govinfo PLAW-119publ111; congress.gov H.R. 5334 enrolled):** "(1) In general.—This title shall not apply with respect to a United States person that is operating under the terms of a general license issued by the Department of the Treasury before the date of the enactment of this Act. (2) Rule of construction.—Nothing in this title shall be construed to affect the terms of a general license described in paragraph (1), the authority of United States persons to continue to operate under such a license, or the authority of the Secretary of the Treasury to extend or issue new general licenses."
- **Sullivan & Cromwell memo (9/29/2026):** §114 "preserves Treasury's authority to extend or issue new licenses"; §§107–108 codify and expand restrictions imposed under EOs 14066 and 14071 (31 CFR part 587).
