NVDA — $12.93B HUGGING FACE ACQUISITION — SEC + COMPANY CONFIRMED
Nvidia signed a definitive agreement to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9303B.
Purchase price to Hugging Face stockholders — approximately $11.9B.
Employee retention program — up to approximately $1.0B.
Expected close — H1 2027, subject to regulatory approvals/customary conditions.
Hugging Face — >18M developers/researchers/creators, >3M models, 500k datasets, 1M applications, >200k companies.
Nvidia explicitly committed to keep the platform open and continue supporting competing silicon vendors.
WHY IT MATTERS — Nvidia is moving farther up the AI stack into the dominant open-model developer/distribution layer rather than relying solely on GPU economics. It potentially gives Nvidia much deeper visibility into model/developer adoption while preserving an open-platform posture to limit ecosystem backlash.
PORTFOLIO READ — Strategically positive NVDA; meaningful regulatory-review risk because of Nvidia's already dominant AI infrastructure position. Intel/AMD were existing Hugging Face investors, but no material financial impact to either has been disclosed.

SEMICONDUCTOR TARIFFS — STATUS ESCALATED FROM PRESS REPORT TO COMMERCE-SECRETARY CONFIRMATION
Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick confirmed the administration is developing a "targeted" semiconductor tariff policy.
Core framework — companies manufacturing in the U.S. receive tariff relief; companies manufacturing abroad should expect tariffs.
Exact rates, covered products and implementation date have NOT been published.
Earlier proposals contemplated potentially extending tariffs beyond bare chips into products such as data-center servers, but that scope is still unconfirmed.
PORTFOLIO READ — Relative positive INTC/GFS and potentially MU/TSM U.S. fabs; potential cost/supply-chain risk for imported semiconductors, packaging and server systems including ASX/DELL depending on final scope.
