# Silicon Data LLM Token Expenditure Index (SDLLMTK) — methodology check, pulled 2026-10-10 ~9:05pm PDT (Exa)
Prompted by Jake ~9:00pm: "I'm betting that weighted average is paid tokens because without $ how do you even measure them?"
- **Silicon Data product page (silicondata.com/products/llm-token-index):** "Each series computes the usage-weighted price of one million tokens across a defined LLM universe. The indices blend active provider pricing with real consumption volume observed across multi-provider routing gateways and inference networks" · "Observations are normalized for input/output mix, context window, then **filtered for models with sustained usage and market expenditure**" · "Models enter the benchmark basket because they show meaningful market activity, not simply because they appear on a provider page" · three views: broad (Bloomberg SDLLMTK), Open LLM, Proprietary LLM (portal).
- **TrakToken (open replica, 7/6):** guesses Silicon Data's usage weights come mainly from OpenRouter; its own replica uses OpenRouter daily top-50 token usage, input/output 8:2, and "**only paid models that enter the daily top 50 with a valid price enter the index**"; notes OpenRouter is mostly independent developers and agent apps, not enterprise direct traffic.
- **GPUQuant docs (SDLLMTK page; secondary, halves taken from Silicon Data's API during a trial):** "Over the 273 days to 30 August 2026 the **open-weight half rose 77 percent and the proprietary half rose 82 percent, and the blend fell eight percent**, because the weight moved: open-weight models went from **16 percent of token spend to 72 percent**. A falling index here is a mix shift toward cheaper models, not cheaper inference."
- **Vynaris (9/7, secondary):** "A usage-weighted index tells you where **paid activity** landed across many meters" · Silicon Data's proprietary expenditure segment −12.1% and open-model expenditure +2.3% (its window; not reconcilable with GPUQuant's 273-day figures — different periods).
