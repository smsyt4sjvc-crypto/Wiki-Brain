# Axios — "The 5 biggest announcements from OpenAI's blockbuster AI conference" (Herb Scribner, 9/29/2026) — pasted by Jake ~9:55am PDT
OpenAI unveiled more than 20 new products and features at DevDay 2026 (Fort Mason, SF, Tue 9/29). Vision: AI that takes on jobs, works in the background, follows users across their digital lives. Comes as OpenAI faces scrutiny over whether autonomous systems can be safely controlled; the company said this week it would not release GPT-6.1 Astra because of security concerns.
1. DOTS — always-on AI assistant "powered by its highly capable Astra model"; users assign tasks/projects, connect to apps, let them continue in the background; competitor to Meta's viral Muse app; "an extension of you" that learns what matters over time.
2. GPT-6.1 SOL — upgraded workhorse GPT-6 that brings advanced capabilities down sharply in price; approaches Astra's performance on coding and computer use "at a fraction of the cost."
3. CHATGPT SPACE — persistent team workspace (Slack + Notion + Drive pieces) with agents embedded.
4. DECISIONS API — companies hand narrow, repetitive choices to AI with a fixed set of outcomes (approve/reject) selected in real time; "AI judgment without unlimited freedom"; compared to TypeFace AI's Jev.
5. CODEX IN THE CLOUD — run the coding agent remotely, start/monitor from a phone; shared dev environments.
Next: AI hardware teased ("worth waiting for"); an agent safety platform similar to Nvidia's newly launched tool. Go deeper: top AI companies probing tens of thousands of security incidents.
