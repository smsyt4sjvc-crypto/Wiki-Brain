# ASML Q3 2026 results (press release, Veldhoven, Oct 14, 2026; via GlobeNewswire/StreetInsider; published ~10pm PT 10/13) — Jake's summary ~10:15pm PDT + primary excerpts

Jake's summary: "Revenue €11.7B, a hair under street's €11.85B but inside the €11-12B guide. Margin 57.5%, above guide. EPS €12.95, big beat. Q4 guided €14.2-15.2B… Full-year raised to €44-45B from €43-45B… the 2028 capacity expansion is still 'investigating,' not planned. None of the bearish ones fired."

Primary (release):
- "Q3 total net sales of €11.7 billion, gross margin of 57.5%, net income of €5.0 billion"; "a record total net sales of €11.7 billion, towards the high end of our guidance"; Installed Base Management €3.3B, above guidance.
- "ASML expects Q4 2026 total net sales between €14.2 billion and €15.2 billion, and a gross margin around 57%"; FY2026 "between €44 billion and €45 billion, with a gross margin around 56%" (July: €43–45B, 54–56%).
- CEO Fouquet: "With such strong visibility and an order book momentum that continues to increase quarter on quarter this year, we now expect that our supply as currently planned for both 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet the demand of our customers. Given this, we are investigating ways to increase capacity for 2028 beyond the 30% increase we previously communicated."
- Footnote: the Q2 release said ASML plans to raise 2026 low-NA EUV and DUV immersion capacity by 30% in 2027, and is investigating a further 30% increase for both in 2028.
- Q2 2026 release (6-K 7/15) quarterly sales: Q3-25 7,516 · Q4-25 9,718 · Q1-26 8,767 · Q2-26 9,326 (€M).
