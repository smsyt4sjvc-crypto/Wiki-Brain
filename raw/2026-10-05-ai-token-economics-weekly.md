Top line: divergence stronger — tokens cheaper while consumption/infrastructure demand accelerate. Silicon Data LLM Token Expenditure Index $0.96/M tokens as of Sept 24, +4.7% over 7 days; FT: >52% below the May 2026 peak (usage-weighted price benchmark). JPMorgan August OpenRouter analysis: token volume +47% MoM, dollar spend +7%, volume-weighted price −28% MoM. Gopher read: model-layer price realization ↓, token quantity ↑, agentic intensity ↑ faster, HBM/memory/compute ↑, routing value ↑. Frontier-margin compression not proven; FT: frontier labs ~70% margin vs ~20% open-weight providers (estimates).
Four demand datasets: (1) ChatGPT 1.2B weekly users (DevDay Sept 29). (2) OpenAI Enterprise Signals: June, 64% of Codex+ChatGPT enterprise output tokens from Codex; top-decile firms 8.3× output tokens/user vs typical (2.6× in Jan); Anthropic Economic Index not updated since June 26. Model economic execution layer: thesis strengthened. Datadog: 69% of orgs use ≥3 models; ~5% of production LLM requests failed (Feb sample), ~60% of failures capacity/rate limits. Vercel: open weights 56% tokens/14% dollars; within Anthropic traffic shift from Fable toward Opus; within OpenAI Sept traffic cheaper models carry volume, Astra captures spend. Unit moving to cost/successful task. No major router M&A/funding this week.

Huawei/DeepSeek: Sept 30 DeepSeek released Ascend-compatible tooling for Ascend 950 (DeepGEMM-Ascend, DeepEP-Ascend), supernodes to 128 chips; claimed 99.8% GEMM utilisation (vendor). Atlas 950 SuperPoD 8,192 NPUs; claimed 4.91M tok/s training, 19.6M inference. Reuters: Ascend 950DT card price raised above RMB250,000 (~$37,255), +20-50% vs two months earlier, HBM costs. No verified Atlas 950 shipment count.
Compute/HBM: Micron CEO (last week): supply/demand tighter through 2027-28; 2027 contracted memory prices "much higher" than 2026. August industry data: memory sales +36%, DRAM +8%, NAND +5%. Reuters Oct 1: Broadcom to provide Anthropic up to $42B of financing for infrastructure; Anthropic expected to be Broadcom's largest compute customer in 2027 — vendor-financed/circular, discount vs third-party cash.
Investable read (gopher): MU cleanest beneficiary; DDOG as public execution/control-plane exposure; AVGO beneficiary and warning sign (financing circularity). Bottom line: AI not less compute-intensive because tokens are cheaper; cheap enough that software consumes vastly more; the valuable decision becomes which intelligence executes which work, when, where, at what reliability-adjusted cost.
