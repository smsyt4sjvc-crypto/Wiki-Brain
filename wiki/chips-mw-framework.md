## WHY THIS MATTERS (plain English)
Chips shipped are not chips working. A GPU earns nothing until a building with power and cooling is switched on around it — and each new chip generation packs FEWER chips per megawatt but produces FAR MORE work per megawatt. So three things must be tracked separately: **chips per MW · useful compute per MW · revenue per MW** — plus a fourth, the falling price of intelligence. The question underneath all of it: does intelligence getting radically cheaper destroy the economics upstream, or does demand explode enough to need more chips, more power and more revenue anyway (Jevons)?

## THESIS — the framework (Jake's project; adopted as the vault's reference)
- **Required AI MW = useful compute demanded ÷ useful compute per MW** ⇒ MW growth ≈ demand growth − perf/MW growth. Examples at 5.4×: 5× demand → MW −7% · 10× → +85% · 30× → +456% · but **30× demand ÷ 30× efficiency → flat.** Neither "tokens exploding" nor "Rubin is 30× better" alone tells you what happens to physical infrastructure.
- **JEVONS SPREAD = useful-compute-demand growth − effective compute/MW growth:** strongly positive → MW, power equipment, networking/HBM ↑ · ~zero → output explodes, infrastructure flat · negative → MW additions slow, GPU rents weaken, capex eventually slows.
- **STRANDED SILICON GAP = delivered/committed accelerator capacity − commissioned operational capacity** (waiting for power, networking, cooling, racks, in inventory, or replacing old gear). Widening = silicon outrunning deployment.
- **POWERED-SILICON ABSORPTION RATE = incremental operational accelerator MW ÷ accelerator MW delivered** (e.g. 8GW live of 10GW shipped = 80%).
- **THE FOUR CURVES:** (1) chips/MW ↓ · (2) useful compute/MW ↑↑ · (3) revenue/MW (monetization) · (4) $/unit of useful intelligence ↓↓↓. **Three price series:** $/token (nominal) · $/fixed capability (real intelligence deflation) · $/GPU-hour or $/MW (the physical asset).
- **The chain:** GPU orders → shipments → GPU-equivalent MW → powered/commissioned MW → utilized MW → workloads → revenue/MW → cash flow.
- **BULL CONFIRMATION:** capability-adjusted price ↓↓↓ AND compute/MW ↑↑ WHILE new operational MW ↑↑, contracted GW ↑↑, new-GPU rents firm, HBM/networking demand ↑, revenue/MW stable or ↑.
- **BEAR SIGNAL:** tokens/tasks ↑↑, $/token ↓↓, compute/MW ↑↑ — but operational MW growth ↓, new GW contracting ↓, GPU rents ↓, HBM/networking pricing ↓, **shipments growth > powered-deployment growth (Stranded Silicon Gap ↑)** ⇒ efficiency outrunning economically useful demand.
- **Current read (Jake's framework):** falling intelligence prices + persistent new-generation GPU scarcity + accelerating power forecasts + enormous agentic workloads = **consistent with Jevons absorption — but operational MW and the Stranded Silicon Gap are the measurements that can prove or disprove it.** *(Vault cross-reads: Silicon Data 9/23 — new-vintage rents rising, H100 rolling over; GS 9/25 — total token demand ~18× vs frontier ~8.6×; Barclays / Texas / Jupiter — the deployment side slipping ⇒ the Stranded Silicon Gap is plausibly WIDENING, unmeasured.)*
