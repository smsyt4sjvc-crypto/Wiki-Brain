SOURCE: ZeroHedge (Tyler Durden), "Russia Admits Renewed Petrol Shortages After Recently Claiming Situation Was
Stabilized", FRIDAY AUG 14 2026 - 05:30 PM. Pasted by Jake 2026-08-14 ~5:40pm PDT.
Cited within: Reuters (3 sources), Interfax, regional outlet 7x7, Russian govt social media post, Krasnodar
governor Kondratyev, Orenburg governor Solntsev, Deputy PM Novak.

- Russia shut one major refinery at ORSK (~800 miles from the front line) for SIX MONTHS after a drone attack
  this week.
- Reuters: "Crude oil exports from Russia's SHESKHARIS terminal at the Black Sea port of NOVOROSSIYSK were
  suspended on Friday following a drone attack, three sources familiar with the matter said."
- Russian government (Friday social post, rare admission): "The situation remains tense in several regions of
  the country regarding fuel supplies to service stations." Oil companies "taking the necessary measures to
  increase deliveries to the most vulnerable regions."
- Krasnodar Oblast governor Kondratyev: "The situation regarding petrol supplies at service stations has become
  complicated." Oil companies "have again started imposing restrictions on the amount of fuel supplied and
  reduced service station opening hours."
- 7x7 count: hours-long fuel lines in at least 12 regions - Krasnodar Krai, Voronezh, Kaluga, Rostov, Ryazan,
  Tambov, Tula, Saratov, Smolensk, Penza, Ulyanovsk, Primorsky Krai. Reports also from Arkhangelsk.
- Sochi city administration, Aug 11: gas stations imposed limits "due to logistical disruptions in fuel supply";
  asked residents and tourists to "refrain from using personal vehicles whenever possible."
- Orenburg region, Governor Solntsev: "difficult situation with fuel." From AUGUST 12 gas stations capped
  purchases at 30 LITRES gasoline / 60 LITRES diesel and adopted an ODD-EVEN system by licence-plate final
  digit. Police to "coordinate the situation at gas stations."
- Deputy PM Alexander NOVAK, end of July (Interfax): "The situation is gradually stabilizing, a number of oil
  refineries became operational again. The balance is better now, and the situation at fuel filling stations has
  considerably improved."
