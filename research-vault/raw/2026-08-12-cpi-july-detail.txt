Headline: CPI-U rose 0.1% in July (seasonally adjusted) and 3.4% over 12 months (not seasonally adjusted). Core CPI rose 0.2% for the month and 2.5% over 12 months. Shelter rose 0.1% and accounted for roughly two-thirds of the monthly all-items increase; energy fell 1.5% in July but remained 14.7% above a year earlier.

9. Notes on how to read the data
CPI-U covers all urban consumers, representing more than 90% of the U.S. population.
CPI-W covers urban wage earners and clerical workers and is a subset of CPI-U.
Seasonally adjusted data are generally preferred for month-to-month trend analysis; unadjusted data are used for 12-month changes and many escalation purposes.
The CPI is an estimate based on sampled prices. BLS reports an estimated standard error of 0.04 percentage point for the 1-month all-items CPI change; a 0.2% monthly estimate would have an approximate 95% confidence interval of 0.12% to 0.28%.
CPI-U and CPI-W are final when released; C-CPI-U is preliminary and revised in subsequent quarters.
