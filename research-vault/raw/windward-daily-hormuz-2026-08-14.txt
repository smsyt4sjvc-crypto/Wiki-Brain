01Strait Traffic Rebounds With Evasive Composition

02Saudi Vessels Divert Around Cape of Good Hope
Twelve Saudi state-linked tankers and bulkers are transiting via the Cape of Good Hope rather than Bab el-
Mandeb and Suez, adding roughly 4,0006,000 nautical miles and 1014 days per voyage, with several
obscuring their true destination until well past South Africa.

A dark VLCC-class tanker began loading at Kharg's western oil terminal on 12 August — the first occupant
since 18 July — while the eastern and LPG terminals remain empty and the offshore waiting-area backlog
holds at 1617 tankers.
