Portable 1MW Kaleidos Microreactor Secures Critical Nuclear Fuel Deal Through 2030s
Aamir Khollam via Interesting Engineering, republished by ZeroHedge. Paste 2026-08-22.

Radiant secured a long-term nuclear fuel source for its Kaleidos microreactor.
STANDARD NUCLEAR will fabricate TRISO fuel for Radiant under an agreement covering planned
Kaleidos deployments THROUGH THE EARLY 2030s. TRISO = uranium particles in ceramic/carbon layers.

KALEIDOS: 1 MEGAWATT microreactor, transportable to customer sites. Operates up to FIVE YEARS
before refueling. Planned operating life 20 YEARS. Transportable by land, sea or air.

Radiant plans to control the broader fuel cycle: customers would NOT handle fresh or spent fuel
on site; Radiant manages fueling, refueling and storage through its own facilities.

TESTING: Kaleidos undergoing full-scale testing at Idaho National Laboratory's DOME facility.
DOE selected Radiant through a competitive process and granted EXCLUSIVE ACCESS TO THE FACILITY
FOR ONE YEAR. Testing at full scale and full power over extended runs, using the same reactor
design and fuel specs intended for customer deployments.

MANUFACTURING: 300,000 sq ft facility in Oak Ridge, Tennessee -- reactor production, fueling,
fuel storage.

CUSTOMERS: EQUINIX has signed a commercial agreement for 20 KALEIDOS UNITS. Also a project with
the Department of the Air Force and the Defense Innovation Unit -- develop and operate a
microreactor at BUCKLEY SPACE FORCE BASE.

Quote: Tori Shivanandan, Radiant president and COO -- "Securing the fuel supply chain is a
strategic advantage to deploying at scale."

Caveat stated in the article: Radiant still needs to complete testing, establish repeatable
manufacturing, and navigate regulatory requirements before widespread deployment.
