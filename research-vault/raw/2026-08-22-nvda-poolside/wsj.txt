"Nvidia Is Spending $6 Billion to Build a Powerful U.S. Alternative to Chinese AI"
Robbie Whelan, WSJ, 2026-08-22 8:00pm. Paste same day. Deal first reported by Newcomer.

TERMS: NVDA invests $1B in Poolside at a $12B PRE-MONEY valuation, AND pays $6B to LICENSE
Poolside's technology and HIRE THE BULK OF ITS ENGINEERS. >100 Poolside employees join NVDA and
work on NEMOTRON. Founders (Eiso Kant, Jason Warner) and ops exec Margarida Garcia do NOT join;
they continue unspecified research.

POOLSIDE: founded 2023 by Kant (developer) and Warner (ex-GitHub CTO). Latest model LAGUNA S,
"one of the most popular open-weight models in the West." Deal came together in the past few weeks,
around the Laguna S release.

⭐ POOLSIDE'S SHAREHOLDER LETTER, THE DISTRESS DISCLOSURE: "At the end of last year, we had a
6 week window in which to raise $2 billion dollars to pay for a 40,000 GB300 cluster coming online
in January. We didn't close it in time, AND WE LOST THE CLUSTER." Poolside said it would run out
of compute as soon as next year without access to capital and data centers; NVDA was "the perfect
partner" because it has "fewer computing infrastructure or cash limitations."

THE RUN-UP THE ARTICLE ITSELF LISTS:
- Nemotron debuted 2023.
- MARCH 2026: NVDA launches the NEMOTRON COALITION -- Mistral, Thinking Machines Lab, Perplexity --
  sharing data, expertise, compute.
- MARCH 2026: WSJ reported Reflection AI (NVDA-backed, "DeepSeek of the West") in advanced talks to
  raise $2.5B at $25B.
- LAST MONTH: Huang's FIRST-EVER X post, a letter "Open Weights and American AI Leadership" --
  the US will be judged "not by one frontier AI model, but by whether the United States builds a
  strong, open ecosystem that diffuses into every sector."
- EARLIER THIS MONTH: NVDA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning; largest version in development, rumoured
  >1 trillion training parameters.

CONTEXT: DeepSeek's Jan-2025 open-weight release caused carnage in US tech stocks; Andreessen called
it a "Sputnik moment." Chinese open models (Moonshot Kimi, Z.AI GLM) now trail frontier US
capability by only a few months.

TENSION THE ARTICLE NAMES: the deal puts NVDA in competition with its own largest customers
(OpenAI, Anthropic) and with other open-model developers it backs. Most large labs are also
designing custom silicon, so they already compete with NVDA.
