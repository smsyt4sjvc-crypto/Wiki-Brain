AMAZON - Q2 2026
  Revenue: $200.6B, up 20%
  AWS revenue: $42.2B, up 37%
  Operating income: $27.5B, up 43%
  Net income: about $62.7B
  EPS: $5.75
  Advertising growth: about 26%
  Stock rose roughly 8% after hours
What mattered:
  AWS growth accelerated sharply and remains Amazon's main profit engine.
  The enormous net-income figure included roughly $53B in non-operating investment
    gains, primarily tied to Anthropic - not recurring operating profit.
  Amazon raised expected 2026 capital spending from about $200B to $220B.
  AI infrastructure demand remains stronger than available capacity.
  Free cash flow is under pressure because Amazon is spending heavily on data
    centers, chips, power and logistics.
Bottom line: Amazon delivered the stronger market reaction. AWS growth justified more
  AI spending, but headline earnings substantially overstated the underlying recurring
  profit because of the Anthropic gain.

APPLE - FISCAL Q3 2026
  Revenue: $109.4B, up 16%
  Net income: $29.8B, up 27%
  EPS: $2.02, up 29%
  Gross margin: 50.1%
  iPhone: $54.3B, up 22%
  Mac: $10.4B, up 29%
  Services: $30.7B, up 12%
  Greater China: $18.8B, up about 22%
  Stock fell after hours despite the beat
What mattered:
  Apple posted its strongest June quarter, led by iPhone and Mac.
  EPS benefited by approximately $0.11 from tariff refunds, making underlying
    earnings closer to $1.91 per share.
  September-quarter revenue growth guidance of roughly 9-11% was below expectations.
  Investors focused on rising memory and component costs, supply constraints and
    softer-than-hoped Services growth.
  Apple's comparatively modest AI infrastructure spending supports cash flow, but
    investors still want clearer evidence that its AI strategy will generate growth.
Bottom line: Apple's reported quarter was excellent, but the market punished the weaker
  forward outlook. Amazon was rewarded for accelerating cloud and AI demand; Apple was
  penalized because growth is expected to slow despite the current-quarter beat.
