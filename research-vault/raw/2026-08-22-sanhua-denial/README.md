WHAT SANHUA SAID (2025-10-15 announcement):
- The rumour that it obtained a large robot order is NOT TRUE (传言不属实).
- There are NO material undisclosed matters requiring disclosure.
- Separately, its securities department told media the robot business was
  "progressing smoothly" but declined to comment on the rumour.

THE RUMOUR IT DENIED — matching the vault's filed claim almost word for word:
- Tesla Optimus LINEAR ACTUATOR order
- ~RMB 5 billion (≈US$685M)   [note: 50亿元 = RMB 5bn, NOT 50bn]
- deliveries beginning Q1 2026
- content for ~180,000 Optimus units

MARKET EFFECT OF THE RUMOUR: Sanhua A-shares hit 涨停 (daily limit up); H-shares surged.
That is the tell — a rumour large enough to limit-up a stock is large enough to require
a clarification, and it got one.
