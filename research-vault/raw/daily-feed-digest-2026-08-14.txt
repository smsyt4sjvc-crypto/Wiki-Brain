IRAN / HORMUZ STANDOFF REMAINS TIGHT
Trump hardened the U.S. position on Iran and described the blockade as effectively controlling maritime access.
Negotiations remain stalled. One tanker reportedly loaded at Kharg Island, but there is not yet evidence of a
broad normalization of Iranian exports.
Trade read: Bullish for refining margins, tanker scarcity and energy volatility. VLO/MPC/PBF thesis intact.

REFINED-PRODUCT SHORTAGE STILL THE BETTER OIL TRADE
Russia remains impaired as a refined-product exporter while Middle Eastern flows remain disrupted. U.S. refiners
are running near capacity and exporting into a tight global diesel market.
Trade read: Favor U.S. refiners over crude producers while diesel cracks remain exceptionally elevated.

JANE STREET HIT BY ~$15 BILLION JULY LOSS
Jane Street reportedly lost roughly $15 billion in July, largely during violent reversals in crowded/momentum
positions. It has since reduced risk in affected strategies.
Trade read: Important warning that AI/momentum positioning is heavily leveraged. Compute fundamentals can remain
strong while high-beta AI equities experience forced liquidations.

AI DATA-CENTER FINANCING SHOWING FRICTION
Nvidia announced a framework intended to mobilize more than $500 billion of outside infrastructure capital, but
that figure is not equivalent to $500 billion of fully committed project financing. Separately, data-center debt
markets are showing wider spreads and slower deal progress.
Trade read: Reinforces preference for GEV / ETN / PWR and physical infrastructure suppliers over highly leveraged
data-center developers.

U.S. CONSUMER DATA WEAKENS
July retail sales disappointed and GDP tracking estimates were marked lower.
Trade read: Negative for discretionary cyclicals. Ordinarily supportive for rates/bonds, but the energy shock
complicates the Fed outlook because expensive fuel can simultaneously hurt growth and raise inflation.

PAYPAL TAKEOVER TALKS ARE REAL
Stripe and Advent have reportedly discussed acquiring PayPal. PayPal previously rejected a $60.50/share proposal
as too low, and discussions may continue at a higher price.
Trade read: PYPL is now an event-driven/M&A trade. At prices above the rejected offer, upside requires a higher bid.

CHINA DRAM PROGRESS IS REAL - BUT NOT YET A MICRON KILLER
Chinese CXMT-based DDR5 has demonstrated increasingly competitive speeds, including enthusiast-level overclocking
results.
Trade read: Long-term competitive warning for MU/Samsung/SK Hynix, but current demonstrations do not establish
competitive yields, cost, HBM capability or mass-production economics.

U.S. REAPER LOSSES SUPPORT DEFENSE REPLENISHMENT
Reporting indicates the U.S. has lost a meaningful portion of its MQ-9 Reaper inventory during the Iran conflict.
Trade read: Supports the broader munitions/drone/air-defense replenishment cycle, although MQ-9 manufacturer
General Atomics is private.

BOTTOM LINE
Near-term priority: Refiners / refined-product scarcity
Multi-year priority: Power generation, grid and electrical infrastructure
More cautious: Leveraged AI/data-center financing and crowded momentum compute
Nothing in today's feed materially weakens the VLO/MPC refinery thesis or the longer-duration GEV/ETN/PWR thesis.
