## EXECUTIVE CLAIM
The Reuters "unexpected winners" story (Generac, transformers, cooling, construction equipment) is
one layer DOWNSTREAM. The first and usually largest checks go to:
1. Regulated electric utilities (DTE, Entergy, AEP, Evergy, co-ops, MLGW/TVA)
2. Independent generators / nuclear operators (Talen, Constellation, Vistra, NextEra)
3. On-site generation providers (Bloom, Chevron/Energy Forge One)
4. Renewable developers (Brookfield Renewable)
5. Powered landlords (Galaxy Digital -> receives CoreWeave lease payments, contracts with utility)
6. THEN downstream to GE Vernova, Caterpillar/Solar Turbines, LG Energy Solution, transformer makers,
   turbine suppliers, EPC, pipelines, fuel suppliers.
KEY: the utility/generator holds the contractual claim on the HYPERSCALER's cash flow.
The equipment maker holds a claim on the UTILITY/DEVELOPER's capex. Different credit, different risk.

## EVERGY - THE TAKE-OR-PAY FINDING
By early 2026 Evergy had signed ESAs with Google, a second Google expansion, Meta, and Beale Infrastructure.
First four = ~1.9 GW steady-state peak. By Q1 2026 a fifth agreement took aggregate signed large-DC peak load
to ~2.5 GW. Large-load structure: customers generally responsible for MINIMUM PAYMENTS COVERING AT LEAST 80%
OF CONTRACTED CAPACITY, plus COLLATERAL REQUIREMENTS and TERMINATION CHARGES.
=> If a hyperscaler overbuilds and walks, the utility does not necessarily lose the expected revenue.

## THE FOUR MARKETS (do not mix)
1. EXISTING generation locked up (Amazon->Talen/Susquehanna; Meta->Vistra; Meta->Constellation/Clinton)
2. EXISTING plants RESTARTED (Microsoft->Constellation/Crane; Google->NextEra/Duane Arnold)
3. COMPLETELY NEW utility generation (Meta->Entergy: >5.2 GW proposed new gas) - biggest 2nd-order capex
4. BEHIND-THE-METER / co-located (Microsoft->Chevron->GEV/Solar; Oracle->Bloom) - avoids interconnection queues

## ⛔ DO NOT DOUBLE-COUNT THE GIGAWATTS
One campus can simultaneously carry a utility-service contract + a renewable PPA + a nuclear contract +
storage + fuel cells + backup gensets. Those MW DO NOT ADD. A 1 GW site drawing from DTE while Google signs
matching renewable PPAs is not 2 GW of load. Correct unit: CRITICAL IT LOAD / PEAK CAMPUS LOAD first, then
ask which resources physically serve vs financially match it.

## THE EVIDENCE LADDER FOR CAPEX REALITY (weight these over ordinary DC announcements)
ESA (customer contractually committed) -> large-load tariff agreement (reveals minimum payments, duration,
termination protection) -> PPA (long-duration generator revenue) -> interconnection/service agreement
(capacity actually reserved) -> state PUC approval (step toward recoverable construction) ->
customer-funded substation/transmission filing (hard physical capex moving) -> gas-turbine SLOT RESERVATION
-> limited/full NOTICE TO PROCEED (EPC/equipment spending begins) -> FINAL INVESTMENT DECISION (project
capital committed) -> EQUIPMENT PURCHASE ORDER (OEM revenue visibility) -> minimum-demand/take-or-pay.
The further down the list, the less speculative.

## WHAT REMAINS HIDDEN
Natural-gas commodity supplier and hedging counterparty - firm pipeline transportation contracts (names/rates
often confidential) - transformer/switchgear OEMs (utilities disclose budgets, not vendors) - EPC contractors
(privately negotiated) - backup generation end customers (the Generac problem) - utility customer names
(regulators sometimes permit confidentiality). => THE PUBLICLY VISIBLE CONTRACTS ARE A FLOOR, NOT A MAP.

## IEA CONTEXT
US data centers get a large share of physical electricity from natural gas, with nuclear and renewables also
important; IEA expects both gas and renewable generation serving US data centers to expand sharply through
2030. IEA also identifies SHORTAGES / LONG LEAD TIMES in gas turbines, transformers and other grid equipment
as a constraint on data-center deployment.

## FOLLOW-THE-MONEY ORDER
hyperscaler commitment -> utility/generator -> generation project -> turbines/fuel cells ->
transformers/switchgear -> substations/transmission -> gas infrastructure -> batteries -> cooling/backup gen.
Reuters describes the RIGHT-HAND side. The leading indicator is several steps earlier, in a utility
commission docket or a generator 8-K.

## THE PREMISE QUALIFICATION
An OPERATING campus necessarily has a power arrangement - but it may be ordinary utility service, not a
bespoke 20-yr PPA. A campus under CONSTRUCTION generally needs a credible energization path, but construction
can begin while later phases, generation additions, regulatory approvals or FID remain outstanding.
Microsoft's 2.67 GW Chevron contract is the illustration: PPA signed, FID expected later in 2026.
