# INBOUND 2026-08-19 — Russian domestic fuel shortage, second wave + Ufa strike
Source: Reuters (relayed by Jake, pasted text). Not independently verified by the vault.

## Retail rationing — purchase limits at the pump, Moscow region
- Gazprom Neft stations: limit 40–60 litres per purchase.
- Tatneft: 50 litres.
- Rosneft: 30 litres of GASOLINE per vehicle — described as nationwide, not Moscow-only.
- Lukoil: acknowledges restrictions in the Moscow region.
- DIESEL is generally LESS RESTRICTED than gasoline.

## Second shortage wave
- Reuters 2026-08-17: shortages RETURNED to at least 10 regions after easing in late July.
- The FIRST wave had spread to most regions by July.

## State response
- Export restrictions on gasoline and diesel.
- Relaxed fuel-QUALITY requirements (spec waivers to stretch supply).
- Petroleum-product IMPORTS.

## Refinery strikes
- Ufa refinery (Bashkortostan) struck again 2026-08-19. >1,000 km from Ukraine.
- Head of Bashkortostan Radiy Khabirov acknowledged a unit hit and damaged pipes;
  characterized damage as minor, repairs "in days."

## Drone scale (RUSSIAN OFFICIAL COUNTS — not independently verifiable)
- Sobyanin: ~637 drones toward Moscow on 2026-08-18.
- ~800 nationally.
