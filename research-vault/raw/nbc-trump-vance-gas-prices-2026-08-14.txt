"How important is the rising cost of gas to White House officials? It depends who you ask.

At a Friday afternoon rally at a police academy in Garden City, New York, President Donald Trump told the
crowd the cost of gas - which currently averages more than $4 a gallon, according to AAA - was 'OK' and not
something he would apologize for.

'For you to pay a tiny little bit more for your gasoline, just remember you're doing it so that a very evil
country cannot have a [nuclear weapon],' Trump told the crowd at the Nassau County Police Academy Center for
Training and Intelligence. 'So remember that when you have to pay a little bit more, you're at $4. It's OK.'

'I'll never apologize,' the president continued. 'I did the right thing.'

The remarks came as a stark reversal from Vice President JD Vance's own comments on Fox News just one day
earlier, when he said reducing the cost of oil and gas was 'goal number one' for the Trump administration.

'That's goal number one, keep oil and gas cheap for Americans all over our country,' Vance told Fox News,
adding that the second goal 'is [to] ensure that Iran never gets a nuclear weapon.'

The president has repeatedly dismissed the rising cost of gas prices, calling the uptick 'a very small price
to pay' to ensure American military success in Iran."
