Proposed framework - four buckets: A Direct AI monetization. B AI infrastructure. C AI financial transmission.
D Independent breadth. Metrics: AI-Adjusted Winning Breadth = economically independent winners / 503; and
AI-Adjusted Outperformance Breadth. Note: revenue exposure alone is not enough - what matters is MARGINAL
EARNINGS exposure. A company can have 10-15% of revenue from data centres while that business accounts for 50%
of incremental EBIT growth and most of the multiple expansion.
Goldman (via Axios): AI investment roughly $600 billion during 2026 - around 2% of US GDP, 10% of business

Bubble implication: not a classic 1999 situation of hundreds of unprofitable companies. A CAPITAL-CYCLE bubble:
one enormous investment decision by a small group of firms generates orders, revenues, financing fees and
valuation expansion through multiple layers. Closest analogy is the housing/credit cycle, not "seven tech
stocks carrying the index."
Warning signal proposed: NOT RSP underperforming SPY. Rather, AI-infrastructure earnings revisions, data-centre
orders and AI-related credit creation rolling over SIMULTANEOUSLY while nominal breadth remains superficially
healthy.
