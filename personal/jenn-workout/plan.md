WEEKLY WORKOUT ROUTINE (GLUTES, LEGS, BACK, CORE & CARDIO) - Body-Solid G9S Home Gym
Equipment: G9S machine, light/medium/heavy tube bands (hook to machine frame or ankles), treadmill, stationary bike, bodyweight
Every session ~60 min. User sets all weights and picks band strength.

HOW TO USE
- "3 x 10-12" = 3 sets of 10 to 12 reps. "/leg" or "/side" = each side.
- Supersets (A1/A2): 1 set of A1, then 1 set of A2, rest 60-90 sec, repeat. Each pair uses only one machine station.
- Weight: last 2 reps of each set should be hard with clean form. Go lighter in week 1.
- Breathing: exhale on exertion, inhale on the return.
- Band anchors: "anchored low/chest height/high" = band hooked to the machine frame at that height. "Hooked to ankle" = band clipped to an ankle cuff. "Stand on band" = band under your feet, handles in your hands.
- Cardio effort (RPE 1-10): Easy = 3-4 | Zone 2 = 5-6 (can talk in full sentences) | Hard = 8-9 (only a few words)
- Belly fat: no exercise burns fat from one spot. It comes off with overall fat loss - the cardio and lifting here plus a modest calorie deficit. The core work tightens and strengthens the midsection underneath.
- Glute Kickback and Leg Abduction (machine) need an Ankle Strap on the low pulley. Use the band set's ankle cuff if it clips onto the cable. Otherwise do the band version listed.

WEEK AT A GLANCE
Mon - Glutes & hamstrings + lats + core + bike sprints 10 min
Tue - Treadmill incline walk Zone 2 30 min + band core & back
Wed - Thighs (front, inner, outer) + mid & lower back + treadmill incline walk 10 min
Thu - Bike 4x4 intervals 28 min + core
Fri - Glutes & legs + lats + core + bike tempo 10 min
Sat - Bike + treadmill Zone 2 40 min + band glute & thigh circuit
Sun - Rest or easy recovery

WARM-UP (Mon/Wed/Fri) - 7 min
1. Bike, easy (RPE 3-4) - 5 min
2. Glute bridges, bodyweight - 15
3. Band lateral walk, light band - 10 steps each way
4. Leg swings, front to back - 10/leg

COOL-DOWN STRETCH (every training day) - 5 min, hold each 20-30 sec, no bouncing
- Glutes: lying figure-4 (ankle over opposite knee, pull legs in)
- Hamstrings: heel on a low surface, leg straight, hinge forward
- Quads: standing, pull heel toward glute
- Hip flexors: half-kneeling lunge
- Inner thighs: seated butterfly, knees toward floor
- Lats/upper back: hold frame with both hands, sit hips back

----------------------------------------
MONDAY - GLUTES & HAMSTRINGS + LATS + BIKE SPRINTS
Warm-up 7 min
A1 Leg Press (machine) | 3 x 10-12 | Feet high and wide on press plate for more glutes; back pad set so knees are at 90 deg; don't lock out knees
A2 Band Pallof Press | 3 x 10/side | Anchored at chest height; press straight out, resist the twist
B1 Lat Pull Down (machine) | 3 x 10-12 | Lat Bar on high pulley; pull to upper chest, never toward head or neck
B2 Band Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 12-15 | Stand on band hip-width, handles in hands; hinge at hips, soft knees, flat back, squeeze glutes to stand
C1 Glute Kickback (machine) | 3 x 12-15/leg | Ankle Strap/cuff on low pulley; face machine, hold on, kick straight leg back. Band version: Band Standing Kickback (anchored low, hooked to ankle)
C2 Band Glute Bridge | 3 x 15 | Lying on back, band across hips, handles pressed to floor; 2-sec squeeze at top
D1 Standing Leg Curl (machine) | 2 x 12-15/leg | Heel under bottom leg pad, knee slightly below top roller pad
D2 Band Lateral Walk | 2 x 12 steps each way | Band clipped between both ankles (or stand on band, hold handles); stay in a half squat
Cardio: Bike sprints 10 min - 2 min easy, 6 rounds of 20 sec hard (RPE 9) / 40 sec easy, 2 min easy
Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
TUESDAY - ZONE 2 INCLINE WALK + BAND CORE & BACK
1. Treadmill warm-up - 5 min easy (RPE 3-4)
2. Treadmill incline walk Zone 2 - 30 min at RPE 5-6
3. Circuit - 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between rounds (~15 min):
   - Resistance Ab Crunch (machine) | 12-15 | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley; Pec Dec Arms moved out of the way
   - Band Standing Row | 15 | Anchored at chest height; pull handles to ribs, squeeze shoulder blades together
   - Band Face Pull | 15 | Light band anchored at face height; pull toward forehead, elbows high
   - Plank | 30-45 sec | Forearms down, body in a straight line
4. Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
WEDNESDAY - THIGHS + MID & LOWER BACK + INCLINE WALK
Warm-up 7 min
A1 Leg Extension (machine) | 3 x 12-15 | Press Arm in Storage position, back pad flat; knees over top roller pads, feet under bottom leg pads
A2 Band Sumo Squat | 3 x 15 | Stand on band in a wide stance, toes out, handles at shoulders (inner thighs + glutes)
B1 Chest Supported Mid Row (machine) | 3 x 10-12 | Chest flat on pad; pull handles until even with midsection
B2 Reverse Lunge | 3 x 12/leg | Bodyweight; step back, back knee drops toward floor
C1 Leg Abduction (machine) | 3 x 12-15/leg | Ankle Strap/cuff on low pulley; stand 1-2 ft away, working leg is the one farther from the machine; hold back pad. Band version: Band Hip Abduction (anchored low, hooked to outside ankle, sweep leg out)
C2 Band Hip Adduction | 3 x 12-15/leg | Anchored low, hooked to the ankle nearest the machine; sweep that leg across in front of the other (inner thigh)
D1 Back Hyperextension (machine) | 2 x 12-15 | Press Arm in Mid Row position, seat at highest position, facing in; hold mid row handles, arms and back straight, lean back from the hips to 45 deg
D2 Dead Bug | 2 x 8/side | Lower back pressed to floor
Cardio: Treadmill incline walk 10 min - steady at RPE 6, roughly 8-12% incline
Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
THURSDAY - BIKE INTERVALS + CORE
1. Bike warm-up - 8 min, building from easy to moderate
2. 4x4 intervals - 28 min: 4 min hard (RPE 8) / 3 min easy (RPE 3), x4. Weeks 1-2: 3 rounds.
3. Core circuit - 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between rounds (~13 min):
   - Oblique Crunch (machine) | 10/side | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley, Press Arm in Storage position; alternate left and right
   - Band Woodchopper | 10/side | Anchored high; pull handle diagonally down across your body to the opposite hip, rotate through the torso
   - Reverse Crunch | 12-15 | Lying on back, curl knees toward chest, lift hips slightly
   - Mountain Climbers | 30 sec | Hands on floor, drive knees toward chest
4. Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
FRIDAY - GLUTES & LEGS + LATS + BIKE TEMPO
Warm-up 7 min
A1 Leg Press (machine) | 3 x 10-12 | Normal foot position; back pad set so knees are at 90 deg; don't lock out knees
A2 Band Pull-Through | 3 x 15 | Anchored low, face away, handles between legs; hinge back, drive hips forward, squeeze glutes
B1 Lat Pull Down (machine) | 3 x 12-15 | Same setup as Monday
B2 Band Split Squat | 3 x 10/leg | Front foot on band, handles at shoulders; back knee drops toward floor
C1 Glute Kickback (machine) | 3 x 15/leg | Same setup as Monday. Band version: Band Standing Kickback
C2 Band Single-Leg RDL | 3 x 10/leg | Stand on band with working foot, handle in opposite hand; free hand on frame for balance; hinge on one leg
D1 Resistance Ab Crunch (machine) | 2 x 15 | Tricep/Ab Strap on mid pulley; Pec Dec Arms moved out of the way
D2 Single-Leg Glute Bridge | 2 x 12/leg | Bodyweight; 2-sec squeeze at top
Cardio: Bike tempo 10 min - steady at RPE 6-7
Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
SATURDAY - LONG ZONE 2 + BAND GLUTE & THIGH CIRCUIT
1. Bike Zone 2 - 20 min at RPE 5-6 (first 3 min easy)
2. Treadmill incline walk Zone 2 - 20 min at RPE 5-6
3. Band circuit - 3 rounds, 45 sec rest between rounds (~15 min):
   - Band Squat | 15 | Stand on band, handles at shoulders
   - Band Standing Kickback | 12/leg | Anchored low, hooked to ankle; face machine, hold on, kick straight leg back
   - Band Hip Adduction | 12/leg | Anchored low, hooked to the ankle nearest the machine; sweep leg across
   - Band Lateral Walk | 12 steps each way | Band between ankles; stay in a half squat
4. Cool-down stretch 5 min

----------------------------------------
SUNDAY - REST OR ACTIVE RECOVERY
Full rest, or: 20-30 min easy bike or walk (RPE 3-4), then 10-15 min full-body stretching with 20-30 sec holds.

----------------------------------------
PROGRESSION
- Hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form -> add weight next session.
- Band moves: when one gets easy, move up a band (light -> medium -> heavy), stand with a wider grip/more stretch, or slow the lowering to 3 sec.
- Track the pin number. Per the chart: Leg Press = 200% of stack, Leg Extension/Leg Curl = 150%, pulleys and press handles = 100%.
- Progress cardio by speed, incline or bike resistance at the same RPE, not by adding time.
- Every 6-8 weeks, take an easy week: same exercises, about half the sets.

SAFETY (from the chart)
- Inspect cables before every session; replace any worn cable before using the machine.
- Check that pop pins and adjustment points are in place and tight.
- Check bands and clips for nicks or wear before each use.
